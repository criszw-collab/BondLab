# Third-party dependencies

BondLab imports these packages; their source and binaries are installed separately through pip, rather than redistributed in this archive.

- QuantLib 1.44: modified BSD (3-clause). https://www.quantlib.org/license.shtml
- FastAPI: MIT. https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi
- Uvicorn: BSD-3-Clause. https://github.com/encode/uvicorn
- Development only, HTTPX: BSD-3-Clause. https://github.com/encode/httpx

Retain the installed packages' license files and notices when packaging or redistributing them. Research citations do not imply endorsement by authors or institutions. Commercial usage rights for any additional data feed must be reviewed separately. Official Treasury evidence and the Federal Reserve data source are identified in validation manifests; the academic papers themselves are linked rather than copied.
