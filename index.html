BondLab combines fixed-income calculations with research-based strategy analysis. Its current pricing engine uses deterministic financial calculations and QuantLib; it does not use an AI model to predict bond prices.

What this engine covers

Regular fixed-rate bullet bonds with explicitly supplied dated date, maturity and settlement. Annual, semiannual or quarterly coupons; Actual/Actual Bond (ICMA), 30/360 Bond Basis, or Actual/365 Fixed. Conventional compound pricing uses QuantLib. Treasury auction pricing implements the regular-period formula in 31 CFR 356, Appendix B, III.D.

Coupon payment dates are contractual and unadjusted. No automated settlement lag, holiday payment adjustment, stubs, ex-coupon rules, callable/putable bonds, TIPS, floating-rate coupons, amortization, default or tax model. Unsupported irregular schedules are rejected. Treasury auction mode requires semiannual Actual/Actual.

Accuracy depends on using the security's actual terms and the correct quote convention. Treasury auction fractional-period discounting differs from periodic compound YTM. Use the auction mode to reproduce published auction prices; do not interchange its yield with another market convention.

All quotes are per $100 principal; accrued interest is added to calculate settlement value. Duration, convexity and DV01 use dirty price at the same yield. Portfolio risk aggregates dollar exposures; no misleading average portfolio YTM is reported.

Research and data

Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen & Vrugt (2018), Carry, motivates the strategy. This app's long-only U.S. maturity-selection rule is an adaptation, not a replication of the paper's global portfolios. Carry per duration is an app extension.

Gürkaynak, Sack & Wright (2007), The U.S. Treasury Yield Curve: 1961 to the Present, provides the real-data source. Zero yields are continuously compounded percentages; par yields must not be substituted.

P(T) = exp[−z(T) × T]
Carry(h) = exp[z(T) × T − z(T−h) × (T−h)] − 1

Last complete observation per month; at least 13 consecutive months. Rank using current-curve one-month carry, then mark the aged bond on the next curve using actual elapsed days/365.25. Interpolate zero yields linearly. Strategy and equal-weight positions are closed and reopened monthly, with the specified round-trip cost deducted each month.

Cash proxy compounds the current one-year zero yield over the holding interval. Volatility and excess Sharpe normalize observations to a month and annualize. CAGR uses calendar elapsed time. Current-vintage curves can be revised; this is not a vintage-correct execution backtest. Maturity selection changes duration risk. Parameter selection after observing outcomes creates bias.

Saved records and deployment scope

Inputs and outputs are recorded in a local SQLite database with timestamps, engine version and SHA-256 hashes. Raw downloaded or uploaded curves are retained as immutable hashed snapshots. Export JSON for reproducibility. These records are not cryptographically signed or tamper-proof.

This release is a local single-user analytics application. Public commercial hosting still requires account isolation, deployment-specific security testing, operational monitoring, backups and data-license review. The included validation report lists what was actually tested.
